Our Story

A Fortress of Hospitality in the Pearl of Africa

Named after the ancient earth-toned architecture that dots Uganda's landscape, The Red Keep offers an intimate escape where traditional Ugandan warmth meets modern comfort. Each corner tells a story of local craftsmanship, from hand-woven bark cloth accents to furniture carved by master artisans in Jinja.
